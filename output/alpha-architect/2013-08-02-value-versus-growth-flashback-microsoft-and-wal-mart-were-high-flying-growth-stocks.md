Jack and I were reading a classic academic paper on value investing the other day: [Contrarian Investment Extrapolation and Risk](http://research.chicagobooth.edu/economy/research/articles/84.pdf).

In this paper Lakonishok, Schleifer and Vishny–the 3 compadres that went on to form the behemoth investment firm [LSV asset management](http://www.lsvasset.com/)–highlight an “obvious” empirical finding for all value investors out there.

The authors find that value stocks earn excess returns because investors over-extrapolate bad news and drive cheap stocks beyond fundamental value.
