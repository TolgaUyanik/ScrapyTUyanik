Memorial Day is a sobering and humbling day for me.

I had the privilege to serve in the United States Marine Corps with young men and women that inspired me everyday.

Unfortunately, some of those inspiring Marines never made it back home.  
Below I highlight a few of the Marines I had the privilege of directly serving with during my time…Semper Fidelis.

All members of the GMF club.
