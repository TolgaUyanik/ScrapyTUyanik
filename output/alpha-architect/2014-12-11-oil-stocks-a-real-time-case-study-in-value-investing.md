### WHO WOULD EVER BUY AN OIL STOCK???

Apparently, nobody.

Energy companies as a whole have been blowing up left and right and now sell at a decent discount to the market.  
[![OIL](https://alphaarchitect.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/OIL.png)](https://alphaarchitect.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/OIL.png)  
Oil specific names are getting killed and they are also getting cheap (at least on an enterprise multiple basis).

Of course, cheap things can get a lot cheaper. Nonetheless, the long-term track record of buying cheap stuff is pretty c[lear](https://alphaarchitect.com//2014/07/01/never-buy-expensive-stocks-period/) (or [here](http://www.millennialinvest.com/blog/2014/11/24/value-and-glamour-investing-re-envisioned)).
