# AA Welcomes the Newest Deadlifter to the Team: Jess Bost

BROOMALL, PA — Alpha Architect, LLC announces that Jess Bost, RICPⓇ has joined the firm as Vice President – Brand Partnerships. In her newly created role, Mrs. Bost will develop and expand upon current RIA relationships to identify, analyze, and build affordable, tax-efficient investment portfolios and assist with the implementation and execution of a strategic plan for empowering investors through education.

![](https://alphaarchitect.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/8c9a0841-f8e2-450b-b21a-1937359a54d90B83085A-E090-4A06-ADFA-9719A97CD6B7-600x547.jpeg)

Jess Bost, RICPⓇ

Mrs. Bost joins [Ryan Kirlin](https://alphaarchitect.com/2017/11/09/formally-welcoming-ryan-kirlin-to-the-alpha-architect-team/) (Head of Capital Markets at Alpha Architect) to build a solution for firms looking to adapt their models to include ETF portfolios built on high transparency and high-quality investment characteristics. Together, Mr. Kirlin and Mrs. Bost will continue to develop investment solutions to educate and empower advisors and investors.  Alpha Architect seeks to capitalize on tax-efficient, lower cost, and transparent ETF portfolios that are customized for advisors unique needs.

Dr. Wes Gray, Ph.D., CEO/CIO of Alpha Architect, states:

> “[Since its inception in 2010, Alpha Architect has been dedicated to its impact mission of empowering investors through education](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xRwycniPGyY). Jess and the other members of our Alpha Architect Team will work as a special ops unit dedicated to proactively providing a network of next-mile support and evidence-based solutions to the RIAs we serve, to ensure the wealth management needs of our advisors and their clients are met with the highest level of efficacy.”

Mrs. Bost launched a [financial planning firm](https://alphaarchitect.com/financialplanning/), Wealth is Strength, in 2020, utilizing her keen understanding of the personal training landscape to create behavior-focused personal finance solutions for fitness-minded individuals wanting to learn how to intentionally build wealth.(1)

![](https://alphaarchitect.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Copy-of-Boutique-Minimal-Logo-5-600x600-1.png)

Prior to that, her rapid growth from Administrative Assistant to COO/Planner of an independent advisory firm helped form her passion for structured planning as the integral piece of building wealth. Throughout her years in wealth management, Jess has also continued as a CrossFit coach and personal trainer. Helping individuals learn to build both wealth and health have become an indivisible part of her legacy and focus.

![](https://alphaarchitect.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/13641178_10153557385611097_2448179450886557153_o.jpg)

Jess coaching crossfit

Mrs. Bost, who has more than 10 years of personal training experience and now four years in the wealth management industry, is also passionate about being an advocate for other women, including the two girls she and her husband are raising. “I was born not just to be strong,” she says, “but strong enough to lend my strength to others too.” She graduated from Berry College with a Bachelor of Science in Psychology.

Jess Bost, Vice President – Brand Partnerships states:

> “Creating a win-win culture based on transparency, evidence, and systematic processes – both for our firm and for our strategic partners – is the path to creating sustainable investors that stay invested and take advantage of the edge created by time and discipline. I have been thoroughly impressed by the integrity and intelligence of Wes and Ryan and others on the Alpha Architect team during our conversations, and even more so by the high esteem their peers have for them. I look forward to working with the Alpha Architect team to ensure we continue to drive premium value for our partners.”
