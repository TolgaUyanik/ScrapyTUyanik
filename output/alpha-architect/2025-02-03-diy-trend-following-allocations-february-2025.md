# DIY Trend-Following Allocations: February 2025

> International equity moving from full hedge to no hedge. Commodities moving from full hedge to partial hedge. Real estate moving from partial hedge to no hedge. Intermediate bonds moving from full hedge to partial hedge.

Do-It-Yourself trend-following asset allocation weights for the [Robust Asset Allocation Index](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/12/02/the-robust-asset-allocation-raa-index/) are available below.

Key Changes:

* International equity moving from full hedge to no hedge.
* Commodities moving from full hedge to partial hedge.
* Real estate moving from partial hedge to no hedge.
* Intermediate bonds moving from full hedge to partial hedge.
