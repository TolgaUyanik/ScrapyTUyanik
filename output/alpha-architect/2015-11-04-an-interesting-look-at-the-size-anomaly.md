# An interesting look at the size anomaly

Many of you are probably aware of the paper from AQR entitled, “Size Matters: If you control for your junk.”

We loved the title so much we considered it one of our [Top 5 Geeky, Yet Funny, Economic Paper Titles](https://alphaarchitect.com/2015/02/24/top-5-geeky-yet-funny-economic-paper-titles/).

Of course, great papers often go unread beyond the abstract because they are a bit dense. A solution to this is to get access to the presentation version of the paper. Typically, a powerpoint deck can convey the information is a more efficient manner than reading through the 50 pages of academic jargon.

Luckily, the Jacobs Levy Equity Management Center hosted an event recently and the AQR crew presented their paper.

Here is a [link](http://181yjyqsa4710psrd2kuip71.wpengine.netdna-cdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/Size-Matters-if-You-Control-Your-Junk-Presentation.pdf) and here is the bottom line:  
[![size matters when controlling for junk](https://alphaarchitect.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/size-matters-when-controlling-for-junk.png)](http://181yjyqsa4710psrd2kuip71.wpengine.netdna-cdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/Size-Matters-if-You-Control-Your-Junk-Presentation.pdf)

The evidence is pretty clear that the size anomaly is alive and well. We alluded to this [conclusion a while ago](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/07/02/does-the-size-effect-exist/)–even without considering quality.
