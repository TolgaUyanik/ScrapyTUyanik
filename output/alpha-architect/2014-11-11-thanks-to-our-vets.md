# Thanks to Our Vets

> I have the privilege to work with 2 partners who served in the Marine Corps from 2004-2008: Captain Wes Gray and Captain Pat Cleary. Both […]

I have the privilege to work with 2 partners who served in the Marine Corps from 2004-2008: [Captain Wes Gray](https://alphaarchitect.com/about) and [Captain Pat Cleary](https://alphaarchitect.com/about).

Both of these guys are Ivy League graduates and both of them lent the best years of their lives to the United States Marine Corps. We also have received venture capital from a former Army Captain. Wes was crazy enough to leave a PhD program to join the Marines–who does that???

<http://www.businessinsider.com/wesley-gray-coming-home-2014-11>[![Wesley Gray coming home - Business Insider_2014-11-11_21-25-20](https://alphaarchitect.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/Wesley-Gray-coming-home-Business-Insider_2014-11-11_21-25-20.png)](https://alphaarchitect.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/Wesley-Gray-coming-home-Business-Insider_2014-11-11_21-25-20.png)  
I’ve been actively involved in Wall Street and business for over 25 years and I’ve learned more about life and business via my veteran partners than I ever did working on Wall Street.

These guys really do stand for honor, courage, and commitment.

Thanks to all the veterans out there–a huge shout-out from Alpha Architect!
