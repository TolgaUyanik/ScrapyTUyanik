# Our Introduction to Alpha Architect Video

> The video maps out our mission, our core beliefs, and explains what we are trying to accomplish in the asset management business.

With help from Dacio Gutierrez, we finished our “Introduction to Alpha Architect” video.(1)

The video maps out [our mission](https://alphaarchitect.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/Firm_Overview.pdf), our core beliefs, and explains what we are trying to accomplish in the asset management business.

The [long-winded written version](https://alphaarchitect.com/2015/01/03/an-introduction-to-investing-and-how-to-use-our-site/) is here, but the video is only 2 minutes — more efficient :-)

Here is the [link to the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xRwycniPGyY).
