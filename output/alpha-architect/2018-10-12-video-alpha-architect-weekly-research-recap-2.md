Today, Ryan and I discuss three topics discussed on our blog over the past two weeks. First, we discuss a post by Wes, discussing the benefits and pitfalls of portfolio diversification. Second, we discuss a new working paper which questions how the Investment CAPM can price momentum. Last, we discuss a guest post by Larry Swedroe, which examines Portfolio Construction for factor funds.
