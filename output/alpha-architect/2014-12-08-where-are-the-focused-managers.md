# Where are the Focused Active Managers?

We highlighted a few weeks ago that [Smart Beta is More Expensive Than You Think.](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/10/21/why-smart-beta-is-more-expensive-than-you-think/)

Smart Beta and other closet-indexers are everywhere, but what happened to old-fashioned high-conviction active management in the mid/large cap space? Last I checked the [market isn’t totally efficient](https://alphaarchitect.com//2014/08/20/inspiring-market-efficiency-results-not/#.VHMzAYvF9MU) and people are [still not 100% rational](https://alphaarchitect.com//2014/05/13/white-paper-trying-hard/#.VHMy4ovF9MV). It seems that there should be some opportunities for those who are prepared to demonstrate some conviction. So who are these intrepid souls?

### Data:

* **ETF:** Our samples of ETFs are from [ETF database](http://etfdb.com/screener/). We sort our samples by categories, *“U.S. Equity, Large-cap, mid-cap, value, growth, and blend”*. After excluding [“fund of funds”](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fund_of_funds) and missing data, our sample include  124 ETFs.
* **Mutual Fund:** Samples of Mutual funds are from [Morningstar Premium Fund Screener.](http://screen.morningstar.com/AdvFunds/Results.html) We use the same sorting options as the ETFs and we get 6,133 samples (missing data excluded).

### Key evidence:

Focused ETF funds (with stock holdings <=50) only accounts for 8% of the funds in the universe. This percentage is consistent with the finding in the article “[SEC Denies Precidian Active ETF Request](http://www.etf.com/sections/features/23604-sec-denies-precidian-active-etf-request.html).”

* On average, most ETFs are lower-cost index trackers, and not actively managed.

There is a higher percentage of mutual funds that are focused (about 23%) compared with ETFs. The average expensive ratio of focused mutual funds is **128 bps**. The more “active” the fund–the higher the cost–on average.

There seems to be a lack of focused ETFs, but a glut of expensive focused mutual funds. When will we see active ETFs finally take off? Maybe never?
