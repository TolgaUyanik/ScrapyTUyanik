> Wes and Jeremy sit down with Kendrick Wakeman (CEO of FinMason) and David Yates (Chief Information Officer at WisdomTree) to discuss the current stat of fintech. The conversation explores the current and prospective technologies that can improve the finance industry. The discussion also wanders into investor education and outlines how and why fintech will be used to solve one of the biggest problems in financial services — helping investors understand why and what they are buying.
