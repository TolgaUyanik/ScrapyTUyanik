# Daily Academic Alpha: Fresh Evidence on the Fama French 5-Factor Model

The past few weeks we’ve highlighted a set of research papers that go back and forth on the validity of the Fama and French “5-factor model.”

A sampling of the research:

* The Fama French [5-Factor Paper](http://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2287202)
* The Kewei, Xue, and Zhang (KXZ) [4-Factor Paper](http://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2520929) (critically assesses the FF 5-factor model)
* Cakici investigates the [FF 5-Factor International Evidence](http://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2601662)
* FF investigate the international evidence for [their 5-Factor model](http://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2622782)

We summarize the debate/controversy in a couple of recent posts:

* [5-Factor vs. 4-Factor](https://alphaarchitect.com/2015/06/10/using-profitability-factor-perhaps-think-twice/)
* [FF International Evidence](https://alphaarchitect.com/2015/07/13/daily-academic-alpha-international-5-factor-evidence-from-fama-and-french/)

Now we have a new paper from [Back, Kapadia, and Ostdiek](http://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2621696) that investigates the merits of both the FF 5-Factor and the KXZ 4-Factor.

### [Alphas of Betas: Testing Characteristics-Based Factor Models](http://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2621696)

> We test the recent Fama-French five-factor model and Hou-Xue-Zhang four-factor model using test assets from Fama-MacBeth regressions, which are bets on particular characteristics or covariances that are neutral with respect to others. Monte Carlo evidence shows that the tests are unbiased, despite errors in variables. Test assets that are bets on characteristics (covariances) have positive (negative) alphas. In particular, **neither model can explain returns related to investment rates**, the **Fama-French model cannot explain momentum**, and the **Hou-Xue-Zhang model cannot explain value**. The rejections are economically significant.
