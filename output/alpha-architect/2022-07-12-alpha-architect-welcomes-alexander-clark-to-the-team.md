# Alpha Architect Welcomes Alexander Clark to the Team

> We are pleased to announce the addition of Alexander Clark to the Alpha Architect team. In his capacity as Vice President – Marketing, Alex will consult with Alpha Architect’s clients to develop strategic solutions that align with their investment philosophy to drive deeper client relationships.

We are pleased to announce the addition of Alexander Clark to the [Alpha Architect team](https://alphaarchitect.com/about/team/). In his  
capacity as Vice President – Marketing, Alex will consult with Alpha Architect’s clients to develop strategic solutions that align with their investment philosophy to drive deeper client relationships.

Alex is an expert in helping financial firms better communicate with their clients. He’s worked with both financial advisors and fund companies to evolve their marketing approach to generate new revenue opportunities.

Throughout his career, he’s been guided by the belief that great marketing should drive down customer acquisition costs while increasing brand affinity; the difference-maker, especially for financial services firms, is to have **education** and **transparency** as bedrock principles.

Alex got on our radar after he helped one of our advisors streamline a presentation that articulated his investment philosophy to his clients. Once we saw the results, we knew Alex had the skills to supercharge our client service model.

While he can’t lift as much as Jess Bost, and he drinks too much coffee to ever have a resting heart rate that can compete with Ryan Kirlin’s, he’s looking forward to working alongside the team to deliver outstanding service to Alpha Architect’s current and future clients.

Personally, Alex and his wife Sarah had the travel bug before settling down with two children. Now, Alex tries to stay cool in Phoenix and chase after his kids.

We’re thrilled to add Alex to the Alpha Architect team and we expect he will add a ton of value to our community and our clients.
