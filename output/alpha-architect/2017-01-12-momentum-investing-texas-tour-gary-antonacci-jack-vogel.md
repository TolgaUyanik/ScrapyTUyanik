# The Momentum Investing Texas Tour with Gary Antonacci and Jack Vogel

> Next week we’ll be touring the great state of Texas. Me, Gary Antonacci, and Jack Vogel, will be traveling between Houston, Austin, and Dallas. Gary […]

Next week we’ll be touring the great state of Texas.

Me, [Gary Antonacci](http://www.dualmomentum.net/), and Jack Vogel, will be traveling between Houston, Austin, and Dallas.

Gary and I will be leading discussions on [momentum investing](https://alphaarchitect.com/2015/12/01/quantitative-momentum-investing-philosophy/). Gary will focus on the use of momentum in asset allocation and I’ll be talking about using momentum for stock selection.

[![gary and wes on momentum](https://alphaarchitect.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/gary-and-wes-on-momentum.png)](https://alphaarchitect.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/gary-and-wes-on-momentum.png)

From left to right: Gary Antonacci, Richard N., Henry B., Wes Gray, Pat Cleary (AA COO/CCO), and Yang Xu (AA capital markets ninja).

And if you don’t want to listen to Gary and I, Jack will be wandering around in the audience talking quant finance until your brain melts.
