Well, it’s my birthday today, so I’m getting older, but I’m in high spirits because younger people are so excited to learn new things.

Over the past week, a few younger blog readers (and even some old pros) have asked about where to find simple, self-contained, introductory finance courses…it just so happens we built one of these a few years ago.

We have an 8-part introduction to finance series, complete with problems and solutions.

* [Class 1:](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/06/30/introduction-to-finance-class-1/) Time value of money
* [Class 2:](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/07/07/introduction-to-finance-class-2-2/) Discounted cash flow
* [Class 3:](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/07/18/introduction-to-finance-class-3/) Bonds and interest rates
* [Class 4:](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/07/24/introduction-to-finance-class-4/) Stock valuation
* [Class 5:](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/07/30/introduction-to-finance-class-5/) Net present value
* [Class 6:](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/08/06/introduction-to-finance-class-6/) Capital investment decisions
* [Class 7:](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/08/12/introduction-to-finance-class-7/) Capital market history
* [Class 8:](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/08/16/introduction-to-finance-class-8/) Risk and return

Once the 8-part class is completed, we recommend you move on to a bit more advanced content at the following link:

https://alphaarchitect.com/2015/01/03/an-introduction-to-investing-and-how-to-use-our-site/

Enjoy. And here’s to [empowering investors through education!](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/09/16/the-alpha-architect-value-proposition/)
