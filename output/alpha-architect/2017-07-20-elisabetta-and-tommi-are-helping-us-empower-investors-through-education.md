# Elisabetta and Tommi Are Helping us Empower Investors Through Education

[Our mission](https://alphaarchitect.com/2015/01/03/an-introduction-to-investing-and-how-to-use-our-site/) is to empower investors through education. This mission is *our passion* and what drives us to go to work everyday.

But we can’t fulfill our mission alone. We need help and our two newest teammates, [Elisabetta Basilico](https://alphaarchitect.com/author/elisabettabasilico/) and [Tommi Johnsen](https://alphaarchitect.com/author/tommijohnsen/), stepped up to the plate.

Elisabetta and Tommi share the same interest and passion as we do.

Their history goes back to the early 2000s when Elisabetta signed up for two “Quantitative Investments” classes taught by Tommi. Their passion for applied asset management research brought them together again, when Elisabetta was hired as a quantitative analyst at the pension fund where Tommi was a consultant. The bond continued after Elisabetta returned to her home country (Italy) and signed up for a PhD in Switzerland. Tommi served as her external PhD advisor on the thesis, which examined the accrual anomaly in Europe.

Their most recent adventure is co-authoring a book on successful factor investing ideas and the research behind these approaches. The book, due at the end of the year, covers a series of topics including: Factor investing, Indexing, ESG, Big Data & AI.

### Elisabetta

Elisabetta brings over 15 years of experience working more recently as an independent consultant in the financial industry advising asset managers and institutional clients on topics like asset allocation, fund and stock picking. Elisabetta graduated with a degree in Finance from *Bocconi University* in Milano, Italy. She attended a dual degree program at the *Daniels College of Business*in Denver obtaining an MSF and MBA degree. While working at Qwest Asset Management (pension fund of a mid-west telecommunications company) she studied and obtained the CFA. Finally, after returning to her home country, she signed up for a PhD program at the *University of St.Gallen* in Switzerland, graduating in 2014. She is known as a bookworm among her friends and had to promise her husband that she would not sign up for any additional degrees. She continues her learning addiction with initiatives like the book writing and blogging. You can follow here at <https://twitter.com/ebasilico>.

### Tommi

Tommi received her PhD from the University of Colorado at Boulder and pursued an academic career at the University of Denver as a faculty member and later as Director of the Reiman School of Finance.  At the same time, she doubled as a consultant, building quantitative investment strategies while pursuing her Olympic skiing ambitions at the world famous ski resorts Colorado is known for.   Tommi brings over 25 years of experience as a teacher, consultant and researcher in the areas of investment management, portfolio construction, and econometrics.  (Oh and by the way, she was just kidding about skiing at the Olympics.) You can follow here at <https://twitter.com/TommiJohnsen>.
