### [Iraq War Vet, Portfolio Manager, and a Tout for Quant Approach to Value Tradition](http://www.nyssa.org/AboutNYSSA/PressReleases/articleType/ArticleView/articleId/186/Iraq-War-Vet-Portfolio-Manager-and-a-Tout-for-Quant-Approach-to-Value-Tradition.aspx)

*Wesley Gray on Authoring Next Gen Graham-Dodd at NYSSA, Jan. 7*

New York (January 2, 2015) – The New York Society of Security Analysts (NYSSA) will host a talk by author, [Iraq War](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq_War) veteran and portfolio manager **[**Wesley Gray**](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesley_Gray)**Wednesday, January 7, about his recent book, *[*Quantitative Value*](http://www.amazon.com/Quantitative-Value-Web-Site-Practitioners/dp/1118328078)*.

Gray, founder of asset manager [Alpha Architect](https://alphaarchitect.com/), will [discuss creating a quantitative strategy of value investing to eliminate behavioral bias](http://www.nyssa.org/programs/upcomingprograms/ctl/viewdetail/mid/1169/itemid/2236/d/20150107.aspx) as part of the #NYSSAauthors Series from 6 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. at the NYSSA Conference Center.

A Ph.D., MBA and finance professor with [Drexel University](http://www.lebow.drexel.edu/), Gray served with the [U.S. Marine Corps](https://www.marines.com/home).  His 2009 book, [Embedded: A Marine Corps Adviser Inside the Iraqi Army](http://www.amazon.com/Embedded-Marine-Corps-Adviser-Inside/dp/1591143403/ref=sr_1_4?s=books&ie=UTF8&qid=1420213932&sr=1-4), is an account of his experience as an [intelligence officer](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_officer) during Operation Iraqi Freedom.

To register to attend, please visit [NYSSA.org](http://www.nyssa.org/programs/mastercalendar/ctl/viewdetail/mid/512/itemid/2236/d/20150107.aspx).  Media can contact [cglynn@nyssa.org](https://mail.google.com/mail/?view=cm&fs=1&tf=1&to=cglynn@nyssa.org) for a press pass.
