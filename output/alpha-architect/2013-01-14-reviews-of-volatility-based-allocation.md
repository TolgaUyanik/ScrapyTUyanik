# Reviews of Volatility-Based Allocation

> My research team at Empiritrage, LLC posted an interesting idea–very new and in need of further R&D–on volatility-based allocation: When Empiritrage released the initial report […]

My research team at [Empiritrage, LLC](http://empiritrage.com/) posted an interesting idea–very new and in need of further R&D–on [volatility-based allocation](http://empiritrage.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/11/VBA_public_v01.pdf):  
![](https://alphaarchitect.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/PowerPoint-Presentation-Google-Chrome_2013-01-14_08-56-12.png)  
When Empiritrage released the initial report we were hoping others in the blogosphere would continue to work on the idea.

Our dream came true:

* First, [CXOAdvisory.com rebacktested the strategy](http://www.cxoadvisory.com/subscription-options/?wlfrom=%2F22378%2Fvolatility-effects%2Ftesting-volatility-based-allocation-with-etfs%2F)
* Next, Meb highlighted the concept in his sweet [“Idea Farm”](http://www.theideafarm.com/research/)
* Finally, [Woodshedder](http://ibankcoin.com/woodshedderblog/), a blog I was previously unfamiliar with, but am now happy to add to my RSS feed, [just did some analysis on the system](http://ibankcoin.com/woodshedderblog/2013/01/14/volatility-based-allocation/).

If you’ve done any work on the system, please share and I’ll post. I know there are multiple ways in which investors can improve upon the VBA system [Empiritrage](http://empiritrage.com/research/) originally explored.
