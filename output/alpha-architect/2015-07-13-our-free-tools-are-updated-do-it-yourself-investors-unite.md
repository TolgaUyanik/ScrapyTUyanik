**Readers:**  
We’ve updated the technology behind [our free tools](http://tools.alphaarchitect.com/) for financial professionals. Unfortunately, this took a long time, but now that we’ve developed the framework, we’ll be able to launch new and better tools in the future.

[![tools alpha](https://alphaarchitect.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/tools-alpha-1030x510.png)](http://tools.alphaarchitect.com/)  
The current core modules are the following:

**Allocation Architect:** This module allows DIY investors to backtest various asset allocation models and receive advanced portfolio analytics.

* Trend-following focus across big-picture asset classes and categories
* [How-To-Guide](https://alphaarchitect.com/2015/02/16/asset-allocation-backtesting-tool/)

[![allocation architect](https://alphaarchitect.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/allocation-architect-1030x514.png)](http://tools.alphaarchitect.com/tools/ma)  
**DIY Investing:** Interesting in running your own active value and momentum strategy? Now you can!

* Monthly updated lists of **top EBIT/TEV**, or “value” firms
* Monthly updated lists of **top 2-12 cumulative return**, or “momentum” firms
* Monthly updated lists for a variety of common tactical asset allocation techniques
* [How-To-Guide](https://alphaarchitect.com/2015/02/17/how-to-guide-diy-investing-tool/)

[![diy output](https://alphaarchitect.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/diy-output-1030x466.png)](http://tools.alphaarchitect.com/tools/diy)  
**Analysis Toolit (Beta):**Leverage top-flight performance analytics and assess the performance of common benchmarks.

* [How-To-Guide](https://alphaarchitect.com/2015/02/16/asset-allocation-backtesting-tool/)

[![output analytics](https://alphaarchitect.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/output-analytics-1030x416.png)](http://tools.alphaarchitect.com/tools/toolkit)
