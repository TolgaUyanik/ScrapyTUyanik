# Meb Faber Podcast: Doug Discusses 1042 QRP and ESOP Transactions

> Doug Pugliese, the head of our 1042 QRP business, was recently invited on the Meb Faber Podcast to discuss the ESOP landscape and the costs […]

Doug Pugliese, the head of our 1042 QRP business, was recently invited on the [Meb Faber Podcast](https://mebfaber.com/2021/02/17/episode-288-doug-pugliese-alpha-architect-if-you-could-sell-your-businesscapital-gains-tax-free-and-roll-the-proceeds-into-a-diversified-portfolio-of-u-s-companies-that-would-b/) to discuss the ESOP landscape and the costs and benefits of 1042 QRP transactions. ([article on the topic is here](https://alphaarchitect.com/2016/12/28/1042-qualified-replacement-property-an-overview-of-esop-rollover-strategies/))

  


---

  

ESOP’s are a niche but incredibly attractive way for a business owner to sell their business for both themselves and the long-term viability of the company. The episode starts with a broad overview and then dives into the inner workings of an ESOP sale. Doug explains how the tax benefits of a 1042 election allow the owner to defer capital gains tax and even invest the proceeds into a portfolio of blue-chip stocks.

As the conversation winds down, Doug shares why ESOP’s may be an ideal way for middle-market private equity companies to exit investments.
