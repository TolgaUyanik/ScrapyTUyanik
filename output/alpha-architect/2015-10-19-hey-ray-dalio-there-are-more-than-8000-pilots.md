# Hey Ray Dalio, there are MORE than 8,000 pilots!

> Ray Dalio, famous for running Bridgewater, the world’s largest hedge fund, is famous for mocking the growth in the hedge fund industry: There are about […]

Ray Dalio, famous for running Bridgewater, the world’s largest hedge fund, is famous for mocking the growth in the [hedge fund industry](http://awealthofcommonsense.com/my-thoughts-on-hedge-funds/):

> There are about 8,000 planes in the air and 100 really good pilots.

Every time I hear that quote I think, “Wow, so many managers are out there trying to get paid big fees!”  
Well, *8,000 is a LOW guesstimate of the planes in the air…*

Turns out there are over **24,000 pilots going** for the “2/20” payment scheme (~10,000 identify as hedge fund specific).

[Here is a link](http://www.sec.gov/divisions/investment/private-funds-statistics/private-funds-statistics-2014-q4.pdf) to the recent compilation of the private fund statements from form PF and ADV data (h.t., Cipperman Compliance).

Here is the table on the # of funds in the private fund space–24,000+! Wow.
