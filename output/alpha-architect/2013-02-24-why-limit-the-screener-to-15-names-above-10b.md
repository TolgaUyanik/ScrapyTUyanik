# Why Limit the Screener to 15 names above $10B?

> Many Turnkeyanalyst.com users and book readers have pointed out that the screening tool is limited to 15 names above $10B, but the book outlines a system […]

Many [Turnkeyanalyst.com](https://alpha.turnkeyanalyst.com/) users and [book readers](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/1118328078/ref=as_li_tf_tl?ie=UTF8&camp=1789&creative=9325&creativeASIN=1118328078&linkCode=as2&tag=welcomtothead-20) have pointed out that the screening tool is limited to 15 names above $10B, but the book outlines a system that holds 30-40 names and invests in stocks that can be much smaller.

### Why the change?

Via [Empiritrage, LLC](http://empiritrage.com/) we manage assets for clients in separately managed accounts. Some of our strategies are directly related to the screening technology discussed in **[Quantitative Value](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/1118328078/ref=as_li_tf_tl?ie=UTF8&camp=1789&creative=9325&creativeASIN=1118328078&linkCode=as2&tag=welcomtothead-20)**.

Due to the large amount of traffic and interest in the Quantitative Value system, the clients of Empiritrage, LLC *strongly suggested* that we limit access to the results from the Quantitative Value algorithm. When the client speaks, we have to listen. Capitalism works.

### Does the Change Dramatically Affect Performance?

In short, not really. Returns drop off 1-2% depending on the assumptions used and the time periods analyzed, but the the punchline is still the same.
