# Calling All Veterans Interested in Finance: How Can we Help?

Today is the [241st birthday of the Marines](http://www.marines.com/home), which is celebrated by all Marines around the world. Tomorrow is Veteran’s Day.

A great time of year to reflect and thank all veterans for keeping America awesome.

As former Marines, we wanted to do a small part in giving back to the veteran community by reiterating that our doors are open to all vets out there.

Basically, if ever have any investing/finance/life questions, you’ve got free life-time consulting from a PhD in finance — so r[each out](https://alphaarchitect.com/contact)!!!

* Want to break into the finance industry?
* Looking to get an MBA or a PhD?
* Trying to [figure out how to invest?](https://alphaarchitect.com/2016/05/26/barracks-cash-a-military-guide-to-better-saving-and-spending/)
* Looking to [translate military lessons](https://alphaarchitect.com/2015/05/25/from-the-frontlines-to-finance-how-the-marines-shaped-our-investment-philosophy/) into investing (see [WSJ piece](http://blogs.wsj.com/experts/2015/11/11/what-the-marine-corps-taught-me-about-investing/) here)?
* Need capital for new business activities?

***We will always make time to help***: no question is too small or too dumb. We may not have the answer, but we can connect you with folks who do.

Semper Fidelis,  
Wes & Pat
