# Housing market repeat?

> I recently came across a few stories about “new” proposals by regulators. Here is the quote from Federal Housing Finance Agency Director Mel Watt: Additionally, Watt […]

I recently came across a few stories about “new” proposals by regulators.

Here is the [quote](http://www.nationalmortgagenews.com/news/regulation/fhfa-to-change-buyback-rules-for-fannie-freddie-1042920-1.html) from Federal Housing Finance Agency Director Mel Watt:

> Additionally, Watt pledged to increase access for less affluent borrowers, saying the FHFA is working with the GSEs to develop guidelines for mortgages with loan-to-value ratios between 95% to 97%. “Through these revised guidelines, we believe that the Enterprises will be able to responsibly serve a targeted segment of creditworthy borrowers with lower-down payment mortgages by taking into account ‘compensating factors,'” said Watt.

What does [Ed DeMarco](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_DeMarco), who was the conservator for Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac from 2009 through 2014 think of the idea?

Sounds like he is not a fan, as evidenced [here](http://finance.yahoo.com/news/housing-expert-says-lowering-down-payments-could-be-dangerous-200215703.html) and [here](http://finance.yahoo.com/news/the-former-head-of-fannie-and-freddie-predicts-their-demise-192653794.html).

As Albert Einstein is credited with [saying](http://ideapharma.com/announcements/the-definition-of-insanity-is-doing-the-same-thing-over-and-over-again-but-expecting-different-resul):

> The definition of insanity is doing the same thing over and over again, but expecting different results.
