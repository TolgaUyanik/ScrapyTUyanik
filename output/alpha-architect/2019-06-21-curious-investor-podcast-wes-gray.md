> AQR co-founder and Managing Principal Cliff Asness and Alpha Architect CEO Wes Gray join *The Curious Investor* hosts Dan Villalon and Gabe Feghali to kick off the podcast’s second season. They discuss momentum investing, or the idea that securities that have done well in the recent past will continue to do well. They talk about what causes momentum and why this factor, which is seemingly simple and widely known among investors, remains a source of returns over the long term.
