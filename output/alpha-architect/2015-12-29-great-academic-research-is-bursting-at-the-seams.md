# Great Academic Research is Bursting at the Seams

> Having been a full-time academic financial economist in a former life (still dabble, when able), I became accustomed to my annual pilgrimage to the annual American Finance […]

Having been a full-time academic financial economist in a former life (still dabble, when able), I became accustomed to my annual pilgrimage to the annual [American Finance Association (AFA) meeting](http://www.afajof.org/). For the uninitiated, the AFA annual meeting is a gathering of all the major brainpower in academic finance. Getting a paper accepted into the program is a big deal for academic researchers (I’ve had the lucky [privilege](http://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2024175) in the past).

One of my favorite activities each year is reviewing all the papers that were accepted for [presentation at the conference](http://www.afajof.org/details/page/8357781/2016-Meeting-Program.html). I’ll typically print the paper list out and highlight all the seminars I want to attend. Unfortunately, this year I won’t be going — too busy with the [business](https://alphaarchitect.com/) and my 3 rugrats over the holiday season. But I still managed to peruse through the 2016 AFA program and highlight some interesting research that will likely be hitting a journal near you…

Here is the [full program](http://www.afajof.org/details/page/8357781/2016-Meeting-Program.html) and here are some links to some papers that looked interesting:

* [How to Squander your Endowment: Pitfalls and Remedies](https://editorialexpress.com/cgi-bin/conference/download.cgi?db_name=AFA2016&paper_id=616)
* [Does it Pay to Invest in Art? A Selection-corrected Returns Perspective](https://editorialexpress.com/cgi-bin/conference/download.cgi?db_name=AFA2016&paper_id=561)
* [Lucky Factors](https://editorialexpress.com/cgi-bin/conference/download.cgi?db_name=AFA2016&paper_id=1495)
* [The Term Structure of Equity Returns: Risk or Mispricing?](https://editorialexpress.com/cgi-bin/conference/download.cgi?db_name=AFA2016&paper_id=225)
* [Longs, Shorts, and the Cross-Section of Stock Returns](https://editorialexpress.com/cgi-bin/conference/download.cgi?db_name=AFA2016&paper_id=1116)
* [Financial Advice and Bank Profits](http://micro.econ.kit.edu/downloads/Financial_Advice_and_Bank_Profits(1).pdf)

There are over a hundreds great papers on the program…check ’em out…
