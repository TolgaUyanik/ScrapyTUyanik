## Focused Factors Index Background

Readers can learn more about the underlying processes of our Focused Factor Indexes via the links below:

* [Quantitative Value Index (QV INDEX)](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/10/07/the-quantitative-value-investing-philosophy/)
* [International Quantitative Value Index (IQV Index)](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/10/07/the-quantitative-value-investing-philosophy/)
* [Quantitative Momentum Index (QM INDEX)](https://alphaarchitect.com/2015/12/01/quantitative-momentum-investing-philosophy/)
* [International Quantitative Momentum Index (IQM Index)](https://alphaarchitect.com/2015/12/01/quantitative-momentum-investing-philosophy/)

The process for the Quantitative Value Indexes can be summarized as follows:

> The Index seeks to buy the cheapest, highest quality value stocks

The process for the Quantitative Momentum Indexes can be summarized as follows:

> The Index seeks to buy stocks with the highest quality momentum.

Active share is the percentage of a portfolio’s stock holdings that differ from its benchmark index. It is based on the weightings of securities in a portfolio compared with those of the benchmark and can be used as a measure of the degree of a portfolio’s active management. For example, an 80% active share means that 80% of the fund’s holdings deviate from its benchmark.

#### **Performance Summary**

* QV gained 13.79% in Q1, bringing the 1 year figure to -5.19%.
* IQV gained 10.75% in Q1, bringing the 1 year figure to -12.78%.
* QM gained 16.20% in Q1, bringing the 1 year figure to -1.81%.
* IQM gained 9.78% in Q1, bringing the 1 year figure to -17.29%.

#### **Performance Commentary**

* How factors contributed to US returns
  + For Q1 2019, beta (market), size, and quality factors outperformed the broad market.
  + While our QV & QM indexes overall outperformed the market in Q1, the value and momentum factors were negative for returns. We still believe owning cheap instead of expensive stocks and strong instead of weak stocks will be rewarded over the long term.
* How factors contributed to International returns
  + For Q1 2019, beta (market), value, momentum, and quality factors outperformed the broad market.
  + IQV outperformed the broader market, whereas IQM lagged. Based off of historical results, it is to be expected that the two indexes are reasonably uncorrelated.
* Additional thoughts
  + Q1 2019 was much better than Q4 2018 and highlights both the pain and the potential gain from investing in focused factor portfolios. We believe size, value, momentum, and quality capture priced economic risks (driven by a mix of fundamentals and behaviors) that should earn expected premia over time.
