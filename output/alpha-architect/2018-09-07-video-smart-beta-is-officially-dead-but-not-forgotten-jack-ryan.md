Ryan Kirlin walks through his experience growing up in smart beta. He was there in the beginning and has seen it to the end. However, now that most investors understand that ETFs are not just passive market-cap weighted portfolios, there is no longer a need to have a ‘buzz’ term that nobody can really define and/or understand.
