# Academic Finance Research Galore. WFA Sessions Announced

Attention all finance geeks. The latest and greatest from academic researchers is available for all to review. The WFA [recently released their sessions.](https://westernfinance-portal.org/program/app/#_BySession) WFA is one of the more prestigious academic conferences and papers presented at the conference often find their way into top-tier academic journals. Highly recommend you check it out!

A few papers that caught my eye as we actively contemplate our decision to be factor-based investors when the only thing working is mega-cap US beta:(1)

* [Noise Trading and Asset Pricing Factors](https://westernfinance-portal.org/viewp.php?n=687932)
* [Common Fund Flows: Flow Hedging and Factor Pricing](https://westernfinance-portal.org/viewp.php?n=714980)
* [The Commodity Futures Risk Premium](https://westernfinance-portal.org/viewp.php?n=655116)
