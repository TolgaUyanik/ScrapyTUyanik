# March for the Fallen Training…in Spain!

> Pat, Brandon, and I are hitting a 35-mile run through the Pyrenees mountains (in Spain) via the Peades dera Aigua. (Thanks to Pat for the motivation!) The event is a preparatory opportunity for March for the Fallen and we will be reflecting on the fallen and the sacrifices of Gold Star families. I will have all my dog tags in tow.

After several COVID years, [March for the Fallen](https://forms.gle/zTsFEkXNLundc8rD8) (#MFTF) is back in full swing this year.

* When: 9/24/2022
* Where: [Fort Indiantown Gap, PA](https://www.gapmwr.com/march-for-the-fallen)
* Why: To show gratitude for Gold Star families.
* Sign Up: <https://forms.gle/zTsFEkXNLundc8rD8>
* More Info: <https://alphaarchitect.com/mftf/>
* We can support logistics (housing/food) for up to 60 people. First come; first serve; sign up above.
