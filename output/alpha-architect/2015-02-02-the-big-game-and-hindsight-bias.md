# The Big Game and Hindsight Bias

After watching the post-game commentary after the Big Game, it appears that many commentators may have been suffering from [Hindsight Bias](https://alphaarchitect.com//2014/05/28/knew-along-bias-hindsight-equal-foresight/#.VM-FwGh4rKM).

Numerous “experts” claimed that the decision by the Seattle Seahawks to throw the ball on the 1-yard line was the “[Worst Call in S.B. History](http://seattle.cbslocal.com/2015/02/01/carroll-seahawks-make-worst-call-in-super-bowl-history/).”

However, when trying to score from the 1-yard line, in the NFL there is a higher success rate when **[passing the ball](http://espn.go.com/nfl/playoffs/2014/story/_/id/12266535/super-bowl-xlix-darrell-bevell-seattle-seahawks-play-call-made-kill-clock)!**(At least for this 2014 season).

With a better throw (aimed at the receiver’s back-shoulder), or a more aggressive receiver (aggressively went for the ball), the Seahawks would have won the game on the slant pattern.

Was it the correct call? From a statistical standpoint, perhaps. Of course, one needs to factor in Marshawn Lynch “Beast Mode” capabilities…

We’ll never know the answer, but things are sometimes more complex than advertised.
